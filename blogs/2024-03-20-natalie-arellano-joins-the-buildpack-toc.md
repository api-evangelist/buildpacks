The Cloud Native Buildpacks team is excited to welcome Natalie Arellano to the project’s Technical Oversight Committee (TOC) . The TOC oversees all aspects of the project and is responsible for defining the long term vision of Cloud Native Buildpacks. Adding Natalie to the TOC helps to ensure the longevity of the project, and recognizes her extensive contributions across many of its components.
